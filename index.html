About Akshardhama Playschool

Our Philosophy

At Akshardhama Playschool, we believe in the power of play-based learning to foster holistic child development. Our approach combines international early childhood education best practices with rich Indian cultural traditions, creating a unique learning environment where children thrive.

We maintain small group sizes to ensure personalized attention for each child, allowing our experienced educators to understand and nurture individual learning styles. Our child-safe infrastructure with CCTV monitoring provides parents with peace of mind while children explore, learn, and grow in a secure setting.

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing, safe, and stimulating environment where children develop a lifelong love for learning through play, creativity, and cultural connection.

Our Core Values

Play-Based Learning

We believe children learn best through play, exploration, and hands-on experiences.

Indian Cultural Roots

We celebrate Indian festivals, stories, and traditions to build cultural identity.

Safety First

Child-safe infrastructure, trained staff, and constant supervision ensure wellbeing.

Individual Attention

Small group sizes allow us to understand and nurture each child's unique potential.

Our Facilities

Our purpose-built playschool is designed with children's needs and safety in mind. Every space is carefully planned to encourage exploration, creativity, and social interaction.

  • Spacious, air-conditioned classrooms with natural lighting
  • Child-safe furniture and learning materials
  • Indoor play area with soft flooring and age-appropriate equipment
  • Outdoor playground with safety surfaces
  • Sleep/rest room for younger children
  • Hygiene stations and child-friendly restrooms
  • CCTV surveillance in all learning and play areas

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the student-teacher ratio?

We maintain a low student-teacher ratio of 1:15 for Playschool, 1:20 for LKG, and 1:25 for UKG to ensure personalized attention.

Do you provide transportation?

Yes, we offer safe and reliable transportation services with trained drivers and attendants. Please contact us for route details and fees.

What are the school timings?

Playschool: 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM, LKG: 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM, UKG: 9:00 AM - 2:00 PM. Daycare facilities available until 6:00 PM.

Is there a waiting list?

Admissions are subject to availability. We recommend applying early as seats fill up quickly. Contact us for current availability.

What safety measures are in place?

We have CCTV surveillance, child-safe infrastructure, trained staff, first-aid facilities, and strict pickup/drop-off protocols.

Do you serve meals?

Yes, we provide nutritious meals and snacks prepared in our hygienic kitchen. Menu is designed by child nutrition experts.
